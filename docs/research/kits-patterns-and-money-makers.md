## What StitchShop should support (ranked)

1. **Recurring clubs and programs (fat quarter club, BOM, kit of the month, mystery quilt).** One model: program, members, monthly cycle, bill date, autopay with card on file, pause or cancel, monthly pick list. The most repeated revenue in this research and the least served by plain POS.
2. **Reserve fabric and kit stock for a program or class** (hold whole-program yardage at sign-up, release on cancel, 5 to 10 percent extra per the other doc).
3. **Kit builder with a bill of materials from stock** that prices the kit, deducts fabric, and records pattern-license cost. Pairs with in-house precuts.
4. **Rosters with deposits and payment plans** for retreats, classes and BOM enrollment, with capacity, waitlist and refund rules.
5. **Pattern records that are cheap to enter**: scan the printed UPC, type the fabric requirements once, shop's own cover photo. No public feed exists (Beyond the Reef says UPCs are printed on its patterns). Do not copy publisher images without permission.
6. **Longarm booking and quilting-service work orders**: reuse the StitchBench work-order model (price per square inch, rental by the hour, certified-customer flag).
7. **Event items**: Row by Row and shop-hop kits and passports as sale items with limited stock and new-customer capture.
8. **Per-item purchase minimums on purchase orders** (small designers use 2, 3 or 5 per design, 12 items per order): show as warnings. Fits Fabric Smarts package F6; nothing here changes that design.
9. **Gift cards and machine sales and service**: needed in a shop POS, but not researched here.
